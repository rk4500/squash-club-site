# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
*   **FLAME University Students**: Active or prospective players looking for an elite, structured sports community on campus to test their skills and compete.
*   **Squash Enthusiasts & Competitors**: Players tracking the official top 20 rankings to challenge opponents or assess tournament schedules.
*   **Recruits & External Visitors**: Potential members evaluating the team's professionalism, athletic prestige, and organizational scale.
*   **Club Committee Members**: Operating executives using the site as a dynamic platform to manage rankings, announce upcoming tournaments, and run recruitment cycles.

## Product Purpose
*   **Elevate FSC Prestige**: Build a premium, elite visual presence that reflects FLAME University's squash competence at a state and national level.
*   **Gamify Campus Squash**: Serve as the official live record of the Squash Ladder, encouraging healthy campus rivalry and competitive drive.
*   **Centralize Club Operations**: Streamline the dissemination of tournament listings, team bios, gallery moments, and recruitment contact points.

## Brand Personality
*   **The 3-word Persona**: Elite, Athletic, Sleek.
*   **Emotional Goals**: Intimidating, highly disciplined, premium-status, and competitively inspiring.
*   **Voice and Tone**: Authoritative, high-performance, minimalist, and energetic.

## Anti-references
*   **Generic School Portals**: Plain white-background, uninspiring, institutional grids.
*   **Over-cluttered SaaS Dashboards**: Excessively complex cards, generic SaaS icons, and boring "corporate-friendly" color schemes.
*   **Uninspired Static Tables**: Traditional, flat Excel-like data presentations lacking visual energy or ranking prestige.

## Design Principles
*   **Court-Ready Tension**: The interface must feel as tense and high-contrast as a squash court, using sharp typography, deep shadows, and stark lines.
*   **The Podium Speaks**: Every ranking element, tournament card, and achievement must celebrate athletic prestige and "Elite Status" visually.
*   **Uncompromising Performance**: Keep page rendering and animations (grain, scanlines, marquee) light, fast, and responsive to align with physical athletic reflexes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
*   **Contrast Standards**: Maintain WCAG AA compliance with the brand's signature gold color palette (`#f5a800`) on deep black/navy backdrops.
*   **Reduced Motion**: Respect system preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`) to scale back or freeze marquee text loops and scanline animations.
*   **Semantic Integrity**: Ensure fully semantic HTML5 tag hierarchy so screen readers can seamlessly navigate player rosters and ladder standings.
